# Incident Report: Cyberattack on Fidelity Services Group (South Africa)

**Source:** [MyBroadband Article](https://mybroadband.co.za/news/security/661700-south-africas-largest-private-security-company-suffers-data-breach.html)

## Threat Landscape Analysis

* **Asset:** Fidelity's databases, which store individuals' personal data and confidential corporate documents.
* **Threat:** Cybercriminals specializing in data theft and extortion.
* **Vulnerability:** Publicly accessible vulnerabilities or compromised employee accounts (e.g., obtained via phishing).
* **Exploit:** Unauthorized access to internal servers.
* **Risk:** Leakage of clients' and staff's personal data (including ID/passport details and sensitive personal information).

## Compromised CIA Triad Properties

* **Confidentiality:** Breach and leakage of confidential corporate data and personal information.
* **Availability:** Certain sections of the network/system had to be isolated and taken offline until the attack was contained and mitigated.
*(Note: Integrity was not explicitly mentioned as compromised, as the focus was on theft rather than data alteration).*

## Threat Actor Profiling (Modus Operandi)

* **Attacker Profile:** The "handwriting" (Tactics, Techniques, and Procedures - TTPs) of this attack strongly suggests the involvement of an **Organized Cybercrime Group**. The fact that the attackers focused primarily on data exfiltration (stealing data) rather than sabotage or destructive behavior (like wiping servers for fun) is a classic hallmark of financially motivated syndicates. They typically steal sensitive information to leverage it for extortion, threatening to leak the data if a ransom is not paid.
